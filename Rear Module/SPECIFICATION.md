## 5. Build and upload

1. Open `rear_module.ino` in the Arduino IDE. If it asks to put the file in a folder called `rear_module`, click OK.
2. Pick the ESP32 board under **Tools → Board** and its port under **Tools → Port**.
3. Click **Upload**.
